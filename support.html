Frequently asked questions

How do I fly?

iPhone and iPad: tap anywhere. Mac: Space, Up arrow, W or click; Esc pauses. Apple TV: click the Siri Remote touch surface or press Play/Pause; Menu or Back pauses. Game controllers work too. Apple Watch: tap the screen or turn the Digital Crown.

How do I restore the Golden Pond Pass on a new device?

Open Coco's Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My progress isn't showing on another device.

Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and iCloud is turned on. Open the game on each device and give it a minute to sync.

What are Duckats and eggs?

Duckats are the in-game coins you earn by flying, finishing missions and clearing stages. Spend them on ducks, hats and trails in Coco's Shop. Eggs are found while playing and hatch into a random cosmetic; the odds are shown in the game. Eggs are never sold for real money.

A stage is too hard!

Every Journey stage can be retried as often as you like, and you keep the Duckats you collect. Practise in Zen mode, where you can't fail.

Game Center isn't working.

Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings, then reopen the game.
