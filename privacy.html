Privacy Policy

Dabble's Journey

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Dabble's Journey ("the game") is made by Aydin Derakhshideh. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We never see who you are or how you play.

Game progress and iCloud

Your progress (Duckats, unlocked ducks and hats, story stars, settings) is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, the game also keeps a copy in your personal iCloud storage using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your progress follows you across your devices. That data lives in your Apple account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service and may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. Game Center is optional and is operated by Apple.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or other billing information. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase is valid so it can deliver your items.

Children

Dabble's Journey is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data at all, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can remove the iCloud copy in your device's iCloud settings (Manage Storage). There is no data on our side to delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
