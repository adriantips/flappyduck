Dabble's Journey

Catch the flock before winter

A storybook flying adventure

Dabble overslept the great migration. Fly through eight hand-drawn pixel worlds, from Mallard Hollow to Sunward Lagoon, to catch up with the flock and get Granny Marigold's scarf back.

  • A full story mode with 48 stages, cutscenes and 12 characters
  • Arcade, Endless, Daily Challenge, Zen, 3D Flight and two-player Duck Duel
  • Ducks, hats and trails to collect, all original pixel art and chiptune music
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch
